Two crashes, two recoveries — and a climb to the top of ASEAN

From the first national car to ASEAN’s #1 market — the shocks that shaped six decades of Malaysian motoring.

1985–94
The national car era
Proton (1985) and Perodua (1994) launch, democratising ownership — annual volume quadruples from ~95k to ~405k by 1997.
1998
Asian Financial Crisis
The market collapses ~60% in a single year. It takes roughly five years to reclaim the pre-crisis peak.
2009
Global Financial Crisis
Barely a dip — just −2%. Malaysia rides out the global shock almost untouched.
2020
Covid lockdowns
Volume falls ~12% — but TIV returns to and exceeds its pre-pandemic level within just two years.
2022
SST tax holiday
Demand ignites. The market sets a new all-time record every year since.
2025
ASEAN's #1 market
Malaysia overtakes Indonesia to become Southeast Asia’s largest car market.
